# UI Review Rubric

Use this rubric when the user wants a deeper review or asks for a score.

## Scoring

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

1 = broken or amateur
2 = usable but weak
3 = acceptable prototype
4 = production quality
5 = excellent, polished, differentiated

## Dimensions

- Clarity: the main purpose and next action are immediately obvious.
- Hierarchy: content priority is expressed through size, weight, position, and whitespace.
- Layout: grouping, alignment, rhythm, and density are intentional.
- Typography: scale, line height, weight, and contrast are consistent and readable.
- Components: controls feel coherent, reusable, and complete across states.
- Interaction: hover, focus, active, loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states are handled.
- Accessibility: contrast, semantics, labels, keyboard navigation, and focus visibility are adequate.
- Responsiveness: layouts adapt cleanly without overflow or cramped interactions.
- Product quality: the screen supports the user decision or workflow with confidence.
- Polish: details feel intentional and avoid generic AI-generated styling.

## Overall Rating

- 0-2.4: not production-ready
- 2.5-3.4: usable prototype
- 3.5-4.2: production-ready with improvements
- 4.3-5.0: strong, polished UI
