# Ultimate Frisbee Terminology Glossary

## Purpose
This document defines which ultimate frisbee terms remain in **English** in both the English and French versions of the app, and which terms get translated.

## Why Keep Some Terms in English?
Ultimate frisbee is an international sport with established English terminology used globally. Many terms don't have natural French equivalents and are used as-is by French-speaking ultimate communities.

---

## Terms That Stay in English

### Game Mechanics
- **Pull** - The throw that starts each point
- **Turnover** - Loss of possession
- **Turn** - Loss of possession (short version)
- **Break** - Scoring on defense (defensive break) or breaking the mark
- **Hold** - Successfully scoring when starting on offense

### Player Positions/Roles
- **Handler** - Player who initiates offense, typically receives the pull
- **Cutter** - Player who runs to receive passes downfield

### Calls/Violations
- **Foul** - Physical contact violation
- **Travel** - Foot fault violation
- **Pick** - Obstruction of a defender
- **Strip** - Knocking the disc out of someone's hand

---

## Mixed Terms (Use English term + French context)

Some UI elements may combine English sport terms with French context:
- "Sélectionner une stratégie de **Pull**"
- "Ajouter un **Turn**"

---

## Notes for Implementation

1. **Translation files**: Mark sport terms with comments like `// SPORT TERM - stays in English`
2. **Consistency**: Use the same terms throughout the app (e.g., always "Pull", never "Lancer initial")
